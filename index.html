Named "the garden of Iqbal" after the national poet Allama Muhammad Iqbal. Before the big wholesale mandi moved out to the Super Highway in 2001, Karachi's main sabzi mandi stood nearby on University Road, and the "Sabzi Mandi" bus stop near Hasan Square still carries the memory.
